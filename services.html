Our Services

Authentic Ifá and Yoruba traditional services delivered with professionalism, care, and deep cultural integrity.

Ifá Spiritual Consultation & Divination

Authentic Ifá divination sessions offering deep spiritual insight, guidance, and clarity on life’s path through traditional Yoruba wisdom.

Ìkọsẹ̀ Wáyé (Traditional Naming Ceremonies)

Sacred naming ceremonies that welcome a new soul into the family and community, rooted in ancestral tradition and cultural significance.

Marriage Blessings & Traditional Weddings

Traditional marriage blessings and Yoruba wedding rites that honour both partners and their families with spiritual and cultural depth.

House Blessings

Spiritual cleansing and blessing of homes and spaces to invite peace, protection, and positive energy into your living environment.

Ancestral Honour

Rites and practices dedicated to honouring ancestors, maintaining the sacred connection between the living and those who came before.

Spiritual Counselling

Compassionate spiritual guidance and counselling to support emotional, mental, and spiritual wellbeing through traditional frameworks.

Herbal Wellbeing Support

Traditional herbal knowledge and wellbeing support drawing on the rich botanical wisdom of Yoruba healing practices.

Yoruba Cultural Education

Educational sessions and workshops that share the language, history, values, and living traditions of Yoruba culture.

Spiritual Items

Carefully prepared spiritual items and tools to support personal practice, protection, and connection to tradition.

Community Events

Gatherings, celebrations, and community events that foster connection, learning, and the shared practice of Yoruba heritage.

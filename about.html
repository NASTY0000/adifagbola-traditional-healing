About Us

Preserving and promoting authentic Ifá traditions and Yoruba culture in the United Kingdom.

Officially Registered

Great News!

Adifagbola Traditional Healing Ltd is now officially registered in England and Wales. This milestone reflects our commitment to operating with the highest standards of professionalism, transparency, and integrity while remaining deeply rooted in authentic Ifá and Yoruba traditions.

Our mission is to preserve, practise, and promote the sacred knowledge of Ifá and the living culture of the Yoruba people within the UK diaspora and beyond. We provide a trusted space for spiritual consultation, cultural education, and traditional wellbeing support.

Whether you seek guidance through Ifá divination, wish to honour your ancestors, celebrate life’s milestones in traditional ways, or simply learn more about Yoruba heritage, we are here to walk alongside you with respect, care, and authenticity.

What Guides Us

Authenticity

We remain faithful to traditional Ifá knowledge and Yoruba cultural practices, never diluting the essence for convenience.

Care & Integrity

Every consultation and ceremony is conducted with deep respect, confidentiality, and genuine concern for your wellbeing.

Cultural Continuity

We work to keep Yoruba heritage alive and accessible in the UK, educating and empowering the next generation.
